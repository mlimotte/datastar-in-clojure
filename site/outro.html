The capstone is the whole of Brew Board in clojure/src/brewboard: the hand-written SSE writer, the menu and pure handle-command, whole-view renders for the customer and the board, the broadcast hub, the reitit routes, and the native-shell routes. Start it and open the two pages side by side (two browsers, or one normal and one private window, so they get different sid cookies):

cd clojure && clojure -M -m brewboard.main      # PORT env var, default 8080
# customer: http://localhost:8080/      board: http://localhost:8080/board

Add a medium oat latte as Ada, place the order, advance it twice on the board, and mark oat milk sold out: every open page follows along, and the customer's drop-down shows oat as sold out without a reload. clojure -M -m brewboard.demo runs the same session headless over real HTTP and prints what each stream received.

native/ios and native/android hold small reference shells, a WKWebView app and a WebView app, that load the same server, apply the served /native/path-config rules, and implement the BrewShell bridge from part 7. They aren't built in CI; native/README.md says what was checked and how to run them against localhost.
